### How I approach a problem

1. **Start from the decision:** what will someone do differently with this data?
2. **Design the pipeline:** how the data is captured, where it's stored, how it's checked.
3. **Measure Before vs After** in business terms (hours saved, errors avoided, dollars), not just model accuracy.
